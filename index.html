The Build

Every villa starts the same way.

Keep scrolling. This is how one plot becomes an ANFAS home.

01 · Design

It starts as a line.

Architects and structural engineers draw every wall, window and step in 3D before a single permit is filed.

02 · The plot

Then it's just land.

We survey the site, set out the footprint and prepare the authority and community approvals.

03 · Excavation

We break ground.

Excavation, soil testing and waterproofing come first, because everything above depends on them.

04 · Foundation

We pour through the night.

Large slabs are cast in one continuous pour, timed for the cooler hours.

05 · Structure

It stands up.

The concrete frame rises floor by floor, with every electrical and plumbing route planned into the slabs.

06 · Facade

Then we dress it in stone.

Travertine, bronze and glass go on by hand, matched to the samples you approved.

07 · Handover

Same ground. Every detail.

One team from the first sketch to the final key.
